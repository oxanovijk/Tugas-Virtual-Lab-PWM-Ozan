# Visual direction

Scientific plotting workspace for Indonesian first-year physics students. The simulation is the primary surface, controls live next to it, numeric results sit immediately below. Broad desktop plotting area and stacked mobile layout.

Deep blue navigation, warm orange projectile and controls, pale green-white paper, subtle coordinate grid. Plus Jakarta Sans is bundled locally under SIL OFL; tabular numerals for measurements. SVG icons share a 1.7px stroke. Geometry on Canvas represents the physical model.

Read mode for theory; operate mode for simulation, experiments, quiz, and journal. Every control has keyboard access, labels, and focus feedback. Charts use strokes and labels as well as color. Motion follows physical time, with explicit launch and pause.
